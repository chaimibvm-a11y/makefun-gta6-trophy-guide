      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy in the whole list is reported as missable. The rest unlock as you play, or stay available in free roam. The game has no mission replay, so the habit below is the whole defence.

      Save habits that protect a run

      • Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.
      • Meditate at Lucia’s balcony spot before you finish Chapter 1: it counts toward Positive Mental Attitude and the spot is gone after the chapter.
      • Leave cheats off on a trophy run: cheats switch off trophies and saving.
      • Finish the side-character final missions (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) before the story ends, even though they stay available.

      Not confirmed yet

      Five trophies have no confirmed unlock method at launch. Their guides say so plainly and give only what the official description implies.
